Background
Lyme Tyme
  • 476,000+ new cases per year in the US alone
  • First identified in the mid-1970s in humans, not livestock
  • Found by Polly Murray, a mother who noticed clusters of children with mystery arthritis and lobbied Yale to investigate
  • Borrelia burgdorferi wasn't isolated until 1982
  • Notoriously hard to diagnose. Rarely fatal — but untreated Lyme carditis (heart complications) can kill
  • Alpha-gal syndrome — a life-threatening allergy to red meat, triggered by certain tick bites
The Facility
Shutter Island I Mean Plum Island
  • Secret US government research facility in Long Island Sound, purchased in 1899 for $90k
  • Officially: studied foot-and-mouth disease and animal pathogens beginning in 1954
  • Core research subjects: ticks, insects, and insect-borne pathogens
  • No public access. Ferry for cleared employees and contractors only, from New London CT and Orient Point NY
  • Owned by DHS (transferred from USDA, Feb 28, 2024). Sale permanently off the table. $18.9M cleanup allocated.
Safety Lapses — On The Record
1978: Foot-and-mouth disease accidentally released into cattle pens outside containment. Bush administration admitted this in 2008.
2004: The virus escaped within the facility — twice in one year.
Government's own records describe a "significant record of containment failures" over decades of operation.

  • Linked Plum Island to Lyme disease, West Nile virus, and duck enteritis — all non-native germs, all first appeared nearby, all being researched on the island at the time

  • Government response: deny everything, call it speculation
  • Led to the Bush administration's 2008 admission of the 1978 and 2004 accidents — under public pressure
  • Carroll today: general counsel of a New York finance company. No mysterious fate.
The Outcome: Moving to Kansas
  • NBAF — National Bio and Agro-Defense Facility, Manhattan, Kansas. Opened June 2023. Cost: over $1.25 billion.
  • Plum Island closes completely by end of 2026
  • Kansas is in Tornado Alley — the government's own risk assessment explicitly acknowledges this
  • National Academies of Science (2010): 70% probability of a foot-and-mouth outbreak from the facility over its 50-year lifespan
  • The Kansas Cattlemen's Association opposed building it there — feared an accidental release would spread "like prairie fire" through US livestock
  • Kansas won because it brought federal money and jobs to the Midwest. Science wasn't the deciding factor.
